About Manna

Manna gives you one Bible passage a day and a few quiet minutes to reflect on it.

Scripture in the English edition is from the Berean Standard Bible (BSB), which is in the public domain.

FAQ

Can I use Manna without an account?
Yes. Choose “Skip for now” to start reading without an account. Some features, such as saving reflections, need an account.

I'm not getting notifications.
Check that Settings → Notifications → Morning reminder is on in the app, and that notifications for Manna are allowed in the iPhone Settings app.

How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings → Account → Delete account in the app. Your data on our server is deleted at the same time.
