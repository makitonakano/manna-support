1. Information we collect

The Manna app collects the following information.

InformationWhenPurpose
Email addressWhen you create an accountAuthentication and sign-in
User ID (@ID)Chosen by you at registrationProfile display
Display name (optional)Any time after registrationGreetings and identification
Favorites and list dataWhile using the appManaging devotions
Device information (OS, model)Automatically (via Firebase)Troubleshooting and improvement

Passwords are processed in encrypted form and are never visible to the operator.

2. How we use information

We use the information we collect only for the following purposes.

  • Account authentication and sign-in
  • Providing app features (showing devotions, saving lists, and so on)
  • Improving app quality and fixing bugs
  • Sending you notifications (only if you turn them on)

    • We never use the information for advertising or sell it to third parties.

    3. Sharing with third parties

    The following services process data for us. They are infrastructure the app needs in order to work.

    ServicePurposePrivacy policy
    Google Firebase AuthenticationSign-infirebase.google.com
    Google FirestoreStoring user datafirebase.google.com
    Sign in with AppleSigning in with your Apple Accountapple.com
    GitHub PagesDelivering each day's devotion to the app. GitHub may record the IP address of the request; no account information is sent.github.com

    Except where disclosure is required by law, we do not provide information to third parties.

    4. Storage and retention

  • User data is stored on Google (Firebase) servers
  • When you delete your account, your sign-in information and your profile data in Firestore are deleted immediately
  • Local data such as favorites and lists is stored on your device in UserDefaults (it can be deleted from within the app when you delete your account)

    • 5. Your rights

    You have the following rights.

  • Access: You can review your registered information in the app's Settings
  • Correction: You can change your display name, email address and password in the app
  • Deletion: You can delete everything from Settings → Account → Delete account
  • Inquiries: Please contact us at the address below

    • 6. Children's privacy

    This app is not directed to children under 13. If we learn that we hold personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

    7. Push notifications

    The morning devotion reminder is optional. You can turn it off at any time in Settings → Notifications, and you can also revoke permission in your device settings.

    8. Changes to this policy

    If we revise this policy, we will announce it in advance in the app or in the GitHub repository. For significant changes we will also notify you by email.
